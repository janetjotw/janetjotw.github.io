**Abstract**

The invention of the Internet is a beautiful and priceless system! The Internet has conquered every household and all classes of society have embraced it. The advent of the Internet, with just a click on the browser, has helped users get information anytime on their device. With the introduction of social media targeting all, it is the youth primarily connected to this engaging platform. Social media are interactive computer technologies that allow individuals, companies, groups to connect virtually from anywhere around the world— can create, share, and view information. This article examines the positive and negative aspects of social media on people and their importance.

**Keywords**: social media, hashtag, millennial.

**Introduction**

Social Media is “the act of engagement,” while social media is the tool used to communicate with the mass audience (Hartshorn, 2010)[[1]](#_ftn1). Social media is the platform that provides individuals to keep connected constantly and with the information exchange happening simultaneously.

**Discussion**

Social media usage has spanned across all ages. But was it just the millennials who were using it? The young and the old developed a devotion to the new system. The introduction of some of the social media apps like Facebook, Twitter, Instagram, YouTube, etc., has made the users hooked on to this new media. This paper discusses some of the positive and negative aspects of social media.

**Positive**

The foremost being information given at your fingertips. An app like Facebook gives you news feed, collaborating groups and pages and the like. The Facebook groups for breastfeeding mothers, baking groups, Moms groups, you name it and there exists a group for everything and what a ton of free and yet valuable information a user gets. The older generation has taken fascination with the newly learned skill—learning to use apps. So, the common observation is that they tend to use these apps to connect to their family members and share photos. It has become a platform to get connected to friends and family.

**Negative**

With every good, comes the evil if not controlled. People are hooked on to these media like never before. The urge to keep checking your status and the likes and posts forgetting the environment you are in; with the onset of news media on apps, there is no Internet Netiquette followed in forums and cyberbullying is on a rise; online predators targeting the vulnerable ones is another serious issue in the virtual world. In addition, a major worry is the clickbait where wrong information or news keeps circulating around social media, just to draw attention and viewership to the site. Therefore, there is no authenticity of the information and hence one needs to be careful.

**Conclusion**

Using social media for the goodness it brings and not getting addicted is the key to the ever-changing technology. A self-realization that brings out discipline in one’s life is essential to using these social media apps wisely and effectively.
